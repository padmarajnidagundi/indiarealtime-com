# Code of Conduct

We want IndiaRealTime to be a respectful, constructive community for everyone.

## Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.
- Do not publish someone else's private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized content, trolling, and sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/padmarajnidagundi/indiarealtime-com/issues). For sensitive reports, open an issue and contact the maintainer without posting private information publicly.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation temporarily or permanently for severe or repeated violations.
